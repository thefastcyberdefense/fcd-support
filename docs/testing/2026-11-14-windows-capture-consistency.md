## Scope Summary

This change stabilizes Windows four-worker process-tree ownership capture and bounded reaping inside [`tests/helpers/process-lifecycle.ts`](../../tests/helpers/process-lifecycle.ts) and adds deterministic synthetic controls in [`tests/process-lifecycle.test.ts`](../../tests/process-lifecycle.test.ts) within the unchanged **5,000 ms** Vitest test timeout and **1,000 ms** shared execution/reap cleanup budget (`500 ms` term + `500 ms` kill). Following independent review `c2d8225` and round-2 validation against `f7e0b6f`, it eliminates the round-1 `fc5bfd8` ownership regressions, removes the `< 250 ms` recent-capture bypass so fresh reap-time WMI/MSYS inspection runs on every cleanup, rejects closed-root unverified native orphans (`test_child_reap_unconfirmed`), invalidates recycled `CreationDate`/`parentPid` WMI rows across capture/prefetch/frontier merges (`mergeWmiSnapshotRows`), avoids four-worker `WmiPrvSE.exe` starvation during `startAutoOwnershipCapture` (`_initialFilterRootPid` + synchronous `settledCapturePromises` tracking), and bounds observer lifecycles (`AUTO_CAPTURE_MIN_TIMEOUT_MS = 1200`) inside caller budgets.

No production scripts, `src/`, `infra/`, `migrations/`, `package.json`, `package-lock.json`, `vitest.config.ts`, assertion strictness, retry/skip rules, native FFI/Job Objects, or Win32 `CreationDate`/group-anchor/canary safety invariants were relaxed or added.

## Remaining Limitations

- **Observer OS child teardown vs synthetic abort proof**: `execBounded` ([`L323-L373`](../../tests/helpers/process-lifecycle.ts#L323-L373)) destroys `proc.stdout`, sends `proc.kill('SIGKILL')`, and resolves immediately on timeout or `AbortSignal`, while `awaitAbortable` ([`L375-L406`](../../tests/helpers/process-lifecycle.ts#L375-L406)) unblocks `captureProcessTreeOwnership` without waiting for underlying promises. The synthetic observer control ([`L1721-L1797`](../../tests/process-lifecycle.test.ts#L1721-L1797)) exercises `AbortSignal`/deadline propagation using fake abortable `stallingOps`; synchronous OS/kernel teardown of real external `ps.exe`/`cscript.exe` child processes within tight caller budgets remains an unverified OS-level boundary rather than proven by that test.
- **OS spawn floor & host contention**: Windows process-tree inspection still invokes external `ps.exe` (`~114–187 ms`) and `cscript.exe` WMI (`~273–420 ms` filtered, up to `~563 ms` under peak contention; `~435–627 ms` unfiltered), leaving an inherent OS process-spawn floor inside shared `1,000 ms` reap budgets. In a four-worker synthetic stress probe spawning a late two-level non-MSYS native subtree after initial capture (`sleep 1.4` followed by `node -> node`), sequential recursive frontier queries plus post-kill native confirmation (`~1,019–1,038 ms`) still exceed `1,000 ms`, failing closed (`test_child_reap_unconfirmed`) rather than falsely reporting cleanup; heavy external multi-core host CPU saturation (such as foreground game/render workloads) likewise pushes `WmiPrvSE.exe` and `setTimeout` scheduling past `1,000 ms`, so clean four-worker timing assumes an uncontended host. No universal four-worker reliability guarantee for arbitrarily deep late-spawned native trees is claimed without Win32 Job Objects or FFI (out of scope here and subject to separate architecture/dependency review).
- **Out-of-scope `health-stdio-docker-harness` margin**: Unrelated `tests/health-stdio-docker-harness.test.ts` ledger cleanup spawns multiple sequential `node.exe` mock-docker processes inside a `3,500 ms` `waitForExit` window and exceeded `3,500 ms` during earlier competing/saturated runs before passing cleanly under finalized solo 4-worker testing (`12.25s` full / `12.45s` focused); it remains untouched as out of scope.
